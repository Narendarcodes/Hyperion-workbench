**Document ID:** HYP‑T01
**Type:** SOP – Pump P‑101 Seal Replacement (synthetic test fixture)

---

### 1. Isolation
Step 1: Apply LOTO on breaker MCC‑3/B‑12 and verify zero energy.
Step 2: Close suction valve V‑101‑S and discharge valve V‑101‑D.

### 2. Seal Replacement
Step 3: Remove coupling guard CG‑101.
Step 4: Install mechanical seal kit **MS‑4471‑K** (qty 1).
Step 5: Torque gland bolts to **45 Nm ± 3 Nm**.

| Item | Part number | Qty |
|------|-------------|-----|
| Seal kit | MS‑4471‑K | 1 |
| O‑ring | OR‑22‑V | 2 |
