SIH26117 · Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited

Confidential work is stuck between two barriers

The confidentiality barrier

Inspection reports, engineering calculations, drawings, SOPs and internal correspondence can't leave the organization — so conventional cloud AI is off the table.

The productivity barrier

Purely manual knowledge work — structuring reports, cross-referencing manuals, verifying claims — consumes engineer hours and loses decisions in the process.

Our operating model

source evidence→AI analysis→explicit inference→human decision

Not a chatbot. The system reduces repetitive knowledge-work effort without transferring accountability for consequential industrial decisions to the AI.

The engine we forked

We didn't build the harness from zero — we forked the best one

hyperion-workbench runs on our fork of DeepSeek Harness, the open-source all-plugin agent runtime. We chose it because the harness — not the model — decides how much of a model's capability becomes completed, reliable work. DeepSeek's own published benchmarks, all run through Harness minimal mode with fixed sampling and maximal reasoning effort:

P0 capabilities

Confidential in, evidence-backed out

Scanned PDFs, photographs, handwritten notes and drawings enter through a controlled local location. Open-weight models and bounded tools do the heavy work. Nothing leaves the boundary.

Multimodal capture

Local OCR and vision turn scans, photos and handwritten notes into inspectable text, tables and regions.

Grounded retrieval

A local knowledge base with version, authority and effective-date metadata. Every claim links to document, page and section.

Model router

Open-weight models registered with capability, modality and compute profile. Tasks route to the smallest model that can do the job — locally.

Sandboxed execution

Calculations and code run in resource-limited sandboxes with no unrestricted network — every invocation recorded.

Risk-based verification

Safety, equipment and numeric claims require human confirmation regardless of model confidence — grouped, not one dialog per claim.

Real deliverables

DOCX approval notes, XLSX outputs and working code — generated with their full evidence trail attached.

Sovereignty monitor

Visible proof, not promises

The PS asks for demonstrable isolation. The workbench exposes live execution state, controlled tool calls, and an event trail that shows sensitive tasks have no uncontrolled external path. Our sandbox plugins follow the same bet NVIDIA made with OpenShell — out-of-process policy enforcement, deny-by-default permissions, metered resources, and a full audit trail of every allow/deny decision.

Status labels describe deployment policy. The environment is validated before any air-gap claim is made.
