Operating model

The work follows
the evidence path.

Nine controlled stages. Every step visible. Every claim grounded.

01

Capture

Source material enters a controlled location.

Documents, data, and instructions arrive through a managed gateway. Nothing bypasses the entry point.

02

Start

An engineer creates a task and links sources.

Every task is a traceable unit. Sources, constraints, and expected outputs are declared upfront.

03

Understand

OCR, vision, and parsing identify content.

Structured extraction converts raw material into machine-readable knowledge — without external APIs.

04

Ground

Local knowledge retrieval attaches evidence.

Every finding links back to its source document, page, and paragraph. No hallucinated references.

05

Analyze

Findings and uncertainty become visible.

Confidence scores, conflicting sources, and gaps are surfaced — not hidden behind a summary.

06

Verify

High-risk claims are confirmed or corrected.

Critical assertions pass through a verification gate before they reach any output.

07

Deliver

A verified artifact is generated.

Reports, analyses, and decisions carry their evidence trail to the output format.

08

Review

A manager reviews evidence and flags.

Human oversight at the decision boundary. Reviewers see the evidence, not the noise.

09

Close

The decision and trace are archived.

Complete audit trail stored locally. Retrievable for compliance, review, or future tasks.

Capabilities

Every surface
does real work.

No decorative dashboards. Each module operates on live material inside the controlled environment.

Document Intelligence

Parse structured and unstructured material into queryable knowledge.

OCR, table extraction, figure analysis, and layout understanding — all running locally against your corpus.

Evidence Grounding

Every claim links to its source. No orphan assertions.

Retrieval-augmented analysis with verifiable citation chains. If it's not in the source, it doesn't appear in the output.

Verification Gate

High-risk outputs pass through human review before delivery.

Confidence scoring, conflict detection, and escalation rules — configurable per domain and risk level.

Controlled Tools

Integrations run through an allowlisted gateway. Nothing calls out without permission.

API access, database queries, and external services are gated by policy. Every call is logged and traceable.

Audit Trail

Complete decision history, retrievable on demand.

Every task, prompt, response, and human action is recorded. Compliance-ready from day one.

Sandboxed Execution

Code and analysis run in resource-limited containers.

No process escapes its boundary. Memory, CPU, and network access are policy-controlled per task.
